Have a question, found a bug, or want to suggest an improvement? Choose the appropriate way to reach the project.

We welcome useful feedback

Background Remove Studio is designed to be simple, but real-world devices and images can reveal issues that are easy to miss. Clear reports help us understand what happened and what needs attention.

Report a problem

For a bug report, please include the tool you were using, your browser and device type, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead. Do not attach private or sensitive images unless you are comfortable sharing them publicly.

Open a GitHub issue ↗

Suggest an improvement

You can also use the project’s GitHub issue page to suggest a feature, report unclear instructions, or share general feedback. Please check whether a similar report already exists before opening a new one.

Visit the project on GitHub ↗

Privacy and image safety

Please do not post passwords, private contact details, confidential documents, or images belonging to someone else without permission in a public issue. For information about image handling and third-party services, read our Privacy Policy.
