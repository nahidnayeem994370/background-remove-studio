Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Background Remove Studio handles images, browser activity, third-party resources, and advertising-related technologies.

Effective date: November 3, 2026

1. Overview

Background Remove Studio provides browser-based image tools. We aim to process the images you choose on your own device and do not operate a Background Remove Studio server that receives images for routine editing. This policy describes the website as it is currently configured and explains what may change if advertising is added.

2. Images you select

When you use the tools, your selected image is read by your browser so the requested editing can take place. The image-processing workflow is designed to keep the image on your device; we do not ask you to create an account or submit the image to us. Your browser may keep temporary image data in memory while the page is open. Downloaded results are saved only when you choose to download them.

Important: The website loads code and AI model resources from third-party delivery services. Those services may receive ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and browser request details, when your browser downloads those resources. This is different from uploading your selected photo to our own image-processing server.

3. Hosting, AI model, and external resources

The website is published as a static site using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses, request details, and security logs when your browser accesses the site. The website may also load JavaScript libraries, AI model files, and fonts from external providers, including content delivery networks and Google Fonts. Those providers operate under their own privacy terms and may process technical connection data needed to deliver the requested files.

4. Cookies and local storage

We do not currently require an account or intentionally use our own advertising profile to identify visitors. Your browser or third-party services may nevertheless use cookies, cache, or similar technologies. Browser cache may store downloaded code or model resources to make later visits faster. You can manage cookies and site data through your browser settings; clearing them may cause resources to download again.

5. Advertising and Google AdSense

At the time this policy was last updated, Google AdSense advertisements have not been added to the website. If advertising is introduced, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar identifiers to serve, measure, limit, and personalize advertisements, subject to applicable settings and policies. Third parties may place and read cookies in your browser or use web beacons and IP addresses as part of ad serving.

For details about Google’s data practices, read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google’s advertising technologies. We will update this policy if advertising or analytics technologies are added.

6. Information we receive

If you contact us through a public GitHub issue, the information you choose to post may be visible to the public and is handled by GitHub under its own policies. Do not post private images, passwords, or sensitive personal information in public reports.

7. Children’s privacy

The tools are general-purpose and are not designed specifically to collect personal information from children. Please do not submit personal information about a child through public feedback channels.

8. External websites

Links to external websites are provided for convenience. We do not control their content, security, or privacy practices. Review the relevant provider’s policy before using an external service.

9. Policy updates

This policy may change when website features, hosting, analytics, or advertising arrangements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the project through the Contact Us page. Please avoid posting private information in public issue reports.
